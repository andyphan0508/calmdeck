Privacy

Last updated: 2026

No tracking

This website has no analytics, cookies or trackers. The only third-party requests are to Google Fonts and unpkg, to load typefaces and icons.

The app

MacCleaner runs entirely on your Mac, including its AI features. It has no telemetry. Secret values found by Secrets Scan never leave your device. Every change it makes is recorded in a local activity log on your Mac only.

License verification

MacCleaner runs entirely on your Mac. No analytics, no tracking. To verify your license, the app contacts our licensing service with your license key only.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by our payment partner, who handles billing, taxes and receipts. We never see your card details. We receive your name, email and order details so we can issue your license and provide support.

Contact

Questions? Use the Contact link on the home page.

Không theo dõi

Ứng dụng

MacCleaner chạy hoàn toàn trên máy Mac của bạn, kể cả các tính năng AI. Không có telemetry. Giá trị bí mật do Quét bí mật tìm thấy không bao giờ rời thiết bị. Mọi thay đổi được ghi vào nhật ký hoạt động chỉ nằm trên máy bạn.
